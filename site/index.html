    How this is counted. A change counts as authored when a tracked engineer is in its Author: or Co-authored-by: trailer; optionally (toggle) also when they are the git author of a commit without such a trailer (a committer's own work). Reviewed counts Reviewed-by:. A fix backpatched to several release branches is counted once. Attribution uses a curated name list, manual lookups, company labels seeded from Percona-Lab/percona-pg-activity (thanks, Percona), and email domains; people with no confirmed employer are listed as Individual contributor. Work by Crunchy Data (now Snowflake) and Neon (now Databricks) before their acquisitions is flagged legacy.
